Full bio

~150 words

Dr. Laura McNeill is a professor of instructional technology at The University of Alabama, where she also serves as an instructional consultant for the university’s Teaching Academy. She holds a Ph.D. in Instructional Leadership with a concentration in Instructional Technology from The University of Alabama, along with an M.S. in Interactive Technology and an M.A. in Journalism and Public Relations.

Before entering higher education, Laura spent six years behind the anchor desk at two CBS affiliates, earning awards from the Associated Press. She is the author of three suspense novels — Center of Gravity (2015) and Sister Dear (2016), published by Thomas Nelson, an imprint of HarperCollins, and The World Breaks Everyone (2018) — and writes award-winning women’s fiction under the name Lauren Clark.

Laura speaks and consults on teaching in the age of AI, engaging course design, educational technology and communication. She lives in Alabama.
