The World Breaks Everyone

Sixteen, alone, and running out of places to hide.

  • Published 2018
  • Genre Suspense

Olivia is sixteen and running. She carries the guilt of her mother’s death and the ache of a father who has disappeared without a word — and she is increasingly sure that whatever took them is now coming for her.

Fast-paced and tightly wound, The World Breaks Everyone follows a girl who has learned that the danger isn’t only to herself. Everyone she lets in is at risk, too.

Laura McNeill Sister Dear A Novel

Sister Dear

Ten years for a crime she didn’t commit. Now she’s coming home.

  • Published 2016
  • Publisher Thomas Nelson
  • Genre Suspense

In 2006, Allie Marshall went to prison for manslaughter in the death of a beloved high school football coach. Ten years later she is paroled — still insisting she didn’t do it — and determined to rebuild a life with the daughter who was five when she left.

But Caroline is a teenager now, raised by Allie’s sister Emma, and the town hasn’t forgotten. Told through the eyes of Allie, Emma, Caroline and the local sheriff, Sister Dear is a story of family loyalty, buried truth, and the price of a second chance.

Laura McNeill Center of Gravity A Novel

Center of Gravity

A perfect marriage. A web of lies. A mother’s fight to hold on.

  • Published July 2015
  • Publisher Thomas Nelson
  • Genre Debut suspense

Ava Carson thought she knew the man she married. But on the Gulf Coast of Alabama, the life she built begins to tilt — and Ava finds herself ricocheted into a web of secrets and lies that threatens everything she loves, including her children.

Laura McNeill’s debut novel is a suspenseful portrait of a marriage unraveling and a mother who refuses to let go.
