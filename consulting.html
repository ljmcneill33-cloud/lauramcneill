FAQ

Good questions

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Most consulting and many workshops happen virtually; on-site visits can be arranged for keynotes, institutes and multi-day engagements.

How are engagements priced?

Every project is scoped individually. After an introductory call you’ll receive a proposal with a fixed fee or a clear hourly estimate.

Can you work with our existing LMS and tools?

Yes. Recommendations start from the platforms and resources you already have, and only add new tools when they clearly serve your learners.

How far ahead should we book?

For keynotes and workshops, four to eight weeks is ideal. Course design projects depend on scope — reach out early so the timeline fits your term.
