Why work with Laura?

Because good teaching is a story well told — and technology should help tell it, not get in the way.

Laura has spent her career helping people understand complicated things. Six years as an award-winning television anchor taught her to make information clear under pressure. A Ph.D. in instructional technology and years as an instructional consultant for The University of Alabama’s Teaching Academy taught her how people actually learn.

That combination shapes every engagement: practical, evidence-based and human-centered. Laura helps faculty and leaders see past the hype, focus on learners, and choose the tools and designs that genuinely serve them.

She brings the same curiosity to every project that she brings to her novels — always asking what’s really going on beneath the surface.

AI Strategy for Teaching & Learning

I help departments and teaching centers move beyond scattered experiments — aligning course policies, assignment design and faculty guidance so AI supports learning instead of short-circuiting it.

Course & Program Design

I help instructors and programs build online, hybrid and face-to-face courses with clear outcomes, aligned assessments and accessible materials — so learners know where they’re going and how to get there.

Faculty Development & Workshops

I design and lead interactive workshops, institutes and one-on-one consultations that leave instructors with strategies they can use the next time they teach.

Keynote & Conference Speaking

I deliver keynotes built for your audience and your moment — moving people from uncertainty to understanding, with a broadcaster’s sense of timing.

Media & On-Camera Coaching

Custom Projects & Support

For needs that don’t fit a single category — a stalled course launch, a new program, a technology rollout — I provide tailored support to get the work moving.
