Training & Speaking

Laura works with educators and leaders who want to use technology well — not just adopt it. Sessions are practical, grounded in real classrooms, and designed to build both skill and judgment. Available in person or virtually, and customized for every audience.

Keynotes

45–60 minute talks for conferences, convocations and kickoffs — delivered with a former anchor’s presence and an educator’s substance.

Virtual or in person · connect for pricing

Host a keynote
  • “Teaching in the Age of AI”For faculty, academic leaders and teaching centers

    A clear-eyed look at what generative AI changes about learning, assessment and integrity — and what stays the same — with strategies faculty can use immediately.

  • “Designing for Engagement”For broad education audiences

    Evidence-based ways to build online, hybrid and face-to-face courses that hold attention and that learners actually finish.

  • “From the Anchor Desk: Communicating with Confidence”For educators, leaders and anyone who presents

    What live television teaches about clarity, presence and staying calm when the teleprompter fails.

Training Workshops

Interactive, hands-on sessions where participants apply ideas to their own courses and leave with a plan.

Virtual or in person · connect for pricing

Host a workshop
  • “AI-Ready Assignments”(90–120 minutes)For faculty and instructional designers

    Redesign one real assignment so it supports learning in a world where students have AI — with clear expectations and authentic assessment.

  • “Writing Your Course AI Policy”(60–90 minutes)For faculty and department chairs

    Draft a clear, fair syllabus statement on AI use, informed by your discipline and learning outcomes.

  • “Online Course Makeover”(90–120 minutes)For online and hybrid instructors

    Apply quality design principles to an existing course: navigation, alignment, accessibility and engagement.

  • “Teaching on Camera”(60–90 minutes)For instructors recording video

    Broadcast techniques for recorded lectures and live sessions — scripting, delivery and simple production that looks professional.

  • “Active Learning, Any Modality”(60–90 minutes)For all instructors

    Practical strategies for discussion, collaboration and retrieval practice in person and online.
