Speaking

A broadcaster’s presence.
An educator’s substance.

Signature Talks

Topics

01

Keynote · Workshop

Teaching in the Age of AI

Generative AI has changed what students can do in a single prompt. This session cuts through the noise to look at what actually changes about learning, assessment and integrity — and what stays the same.

  • A clear-eyed framework for when AI helps learning and when it gets in the way
  • Assignment redesign strategies faculty can apply immediately
  • Starting points for course and campus AI guidelines
02

Keynote · Workshop

Designing for Engagement

Whether a course is online, hybrid or face-to-face, attention is earned. Drawing on instructional design research and years of faculty development, Laura shares practical ways to build courses learners want to finish.

  • Evidence-based design moves for any modality
  • Using video, media and interaction with purpose
  • Quick wins for existing courses
03

Keynote · Training

From the Anchor Desk: Communicating with Confidence

What live television teaches about clarity, presence and staying calm when the teleprompter fails. Built for educators, leaders and anyone who presents, records or teaches on camera.

  • On-camera and presentation techniques from broadcast news
  • Structuring a message people remember
  • Practical tips for recorded lectures and video
04

Author Talk

Secrets, Lies & Southern Settings

For libraries, book festivals and reader events: Laura talks about writing suspense, the journey from newsroom to novelist, and why every family has a story it would rather not tell.

  • Behind the scenes of Center of Gravity, Sister Dear and The World Breaks Everyone
  • Writing under two names in two genres
  • Q&A and signing
