Biography

A journalist’s instinct for the story behind the story.

Laura McNeill spent six years behind the anchor desk at two CBS affiliates, where her reporting earned awards from the Associated Press. Years in the newsroom taught her that the most compelling stories are rarely the ones on the surface.

She moved to the Alabama Gulf Coast to raise her family and write novels. Her debut suspense novel, Center of Gravity, was published by Thomas Nelson, an imprint of HarperCollins, in 2015, followed by Sister Dear in 2016 and The World Breaks Everyone in 2018.

Writing as Lauren Clark, she is also the author of award-winning women’s fiction, including Dancing Naked in Dixie, Stardust Summer, Pie Girls and Stay Tuned.

Today Laura is a professor of instructional technology at The University of Alabama, where she also serves as an instructional consultant for the university’s Teaching Academy — helping faculty tell better stories in the classroom.
