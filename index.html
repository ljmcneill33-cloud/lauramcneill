About Laura

From the anchor desk to the dark side of the Gulf Coast.

Before she wrote suspense, Laura McNeill spent six years behind the anchor desk at two CBS affiliates, earning awards from the Associated Press. She traded breaking news for the Alabama Gulf Coast, a family, and the stories that wouldn’t leave her alone.

Today she writes novels, teaches as a professor of instructional technology at The University of Alabama, and pens women’s fiction under the name Lauren Clark.
